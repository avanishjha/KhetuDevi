Our Story

Not the story of an institution. The story of a family.

The story of the Khetu Devi Memorial Trust is the story of a family that rose from hardship through courage, honest work, faith, sacrifice and compassion — and chose to turn its blessings into service for others.

Rooted in the soil of Rajasthan and shaped by years of struggle, the family's journey reflects simplicity, responsibility, perseverance, devotion, generosity and humanity. At the heart of this legacy stands Late Smt. Khetu Devi, whose life became an enduring example of a mother's love, strength and selfless service.

The Trust carries her memory forward through meaningful action — supporting education, healthcare, the environment, social welfare and the preservation of spiritual and cultural values.

The Journey Behind the Legacy

Two families, two villages, and the generations that followed

The family's roots trace back to Bhadkha village. Shri Ramdan Ji Godara came from Kudsi village in Bikaner and settled in Bhadkha. Around the same period, Shri Jetaram Saran came from Bikaner and settled in Bhimda village. His daughter Jasodaji married Shri Ramdan Ji, and their family grew through the generations.

The early years were marked by loss. Deepaji Ji, one of the sons, lost his parents young and shouldered family responsibilities early in life, raised in his maternal family — the Sau family — amidst poverty and difficulty.

At seventeen he was married to Smt. Khetu Devi, daughter of Shri Gokla Ram Ji Jakhar and Smt. Yashoda Devi. She had known the same losses: her mother died when she was seven, her father when she was twelve. As the eldest, she took on responsibilities far beyond her years — managing the household, caring for livestock, working the land.

Deepaji Ji worked as a labourer and farmer. When he later received a railway job, he gave the opportunity to his younger brother Kanaram Ji instead — placing family welfare above his own advancement. Together they raised six children: Chunnilal, Poonam Ram, Kasturi Devi, Narayan Ram, Uma Ram and Heera Ram.

A Mother's Faith

She vowed that until her son came home she would not wear slippers. She kept that vow for three years, barefoot.

In 1974, her eldest son Chunnilal left school while studying in Class 10 because of poverty and difficult circumstances. Under the guidance of his Guru, Shri Chandan Singh Ji Rao, he travelled to Raichur in Karnataka in search of work. His departure caused deep anguish. There were fears, questions and social criticism. People even taunted his mother, suggesting that poverty had forced her to send her son away. She refused to surrender to despair.

From Struggle to Prosperity

What perseverance, unity and honest work can build

Chunnilal Ji spent four years working in Karnataka. Through hard work he earned money and established his own clothing business in Siruguppa, Ballari district, before returning to his village. With time, every member of the family worked diligently in their respective fields and established businesses and institutions.

When Bhagyodaya Rice Industry was inaugurated, the family's wish was that Khetu Devi herself should press the button. The business began with her hands. Prosperity has meaning only when it stays connected to gratitude, family values and service.

Her Continuing Presence

Her presence ended. Her influence did not.

Her youngest son, Shri Heera Ram Ji Godara, remembers his mother as invaluable to the family. Her motherhood, patience, hard work, spirit of service, honesty, contentment, generosity and warmth continue to guide them. He describes her as a source of light and blessing — a mother whose life itself was a form of worship.

On 31 July 2020, Khetu Devi passed away. In her memory the family chose to walk the path she showed them: human service, charity, compassion, hard work and honesty.

Every year on 31 July her death anniversary is observed at Khariya Tala, Bhadkha — an all-night devotional gathering, the blessings of saints, and a large community bhandara.

A Special Initiative

Bringing “Maa Jeevan Hai” to every family

This year the Dipkani family has undertaken a special initiative to take the widely appreciated book “Maa Jeevan Hai”, written by Shri Kamlesh Chandra, to people across the country.

The vision is to connect every family with the values of service, संस्कार and the greatness of motherhood through the power of the written word.

The book brings together perspectives from ancient traditions, religious scriptures, Upanishads, Vedas, Puranas, great literary thinkers and scientific viewpoints — helping a new generation understand the significance of a mother.

Our Philosophy

Where service becomes a resolve, the welfare of society becomes inevitable.

True tribute is not built through monuments alone. It is built when a life of sacrifice inspires another act of kindness; when a student's dream is protected; when a patient's family finds a place to rest; when a mother's memory becomes a scholarship; and when prosperity becomes a means of giving back.

Khetu Devi's life began amid hardship, but her legacy reaches far beyond it. Every initiative is guided by the same values that shaped her life: work honestly, live simply, give generously, care deeply, and serve without expectation.
